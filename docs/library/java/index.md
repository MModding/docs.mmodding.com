This part of the documentation is focused on utility objects that do not depend on Minecraft's content.

This module evolves occasionally, when MModding devs think of things to abstract.

Let's give a list of these objects. Those will be detailed in their respective entries:

- The [`Color`](./colors.md) object and their extensions.
- The container objects such as `Unit`, `Pair`, etc...
- The [`Either`](./eithers.md) object.
- The [functional interfaces](./finterfaces).
- The [list variants](./lists).
- The [map variants](./maps).
- The [object property interfaces](./opinterfaces).
